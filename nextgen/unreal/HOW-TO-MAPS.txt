DriveAnywhere — how to open other maps in Unreal
================================================

Content Browser → DriveAnywhere → Maps

Double-click any of:
  MAP_WestminsterSprint
  MAP_EmbankmentRun
  MAP_CanaryWharfLoop
  MAP_DubaiMarinaCircuit
  MAP_EgyptPyramids
  MAP_NewYorkHarborCircuit
  MAP_TokyoDriftCircuit
  MAP_AlpsMountainPass
  MAP_RioCoastCircuit

Then Alt+P to play.

Each map now has:
  - up to 30 unique labelled silhouettes (Eye, Burj, Empire kinds, etc.)
  - all named building labels from the web routes
  - city heroes (Big Ben / Burj / Empire / Cristo / …)
  - Tecpro barriers, trees, signs, correct spawn

Host-only car/track/AI is already on the web lobby (room-lobby.tsx).
